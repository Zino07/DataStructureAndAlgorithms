The Noor house

Noor is a small-batch thobe house. Fewer, better pieces — honest fabrics, quiet details, no compromise.

Cut for real life

Every thobe is cut for easy movement and finished with double-stitched seams that survive daily wear and Friday crowds.

Fabric first

Breathable cotton, linen blends and soft crepe. No scratchy synthetics, no see-through surprises in daylight.

Small weekly drops

Released in small batches so colours stay fresh, stock stays honest, and every piece is checked before it ships.

How sizing works

Algerian thobes are numbered 52–58 by height. 52 fits roughly 165–170 cm, 58 fits 180–186 cm. If you are between sizes, choose the larger — the drape stays clean and the shoulders stay comfortable.

Noor · fit guide

Find your thobe size

Algerian thobes are numbered by height. Measure yourself in centimetres, then choose the closest size. If you are between two sizes, size up.

SizeHeightChestThobe lengthSleeve
52160–168 cm96–100 cm138 cm60 cm
54168–174 cm100–104 cm142 cm62 cm
56174–180 cm104–108 cm146 cm64 cm
58180–186 cm108–114 cm150 cm66 cm
1 · HeightStand straight against a wall, without shoes. This is the main size guide.
2 · ChestMeasure around the fullest part, keeping the tape level and relaxed.
3 · LengthFrom the shoulder seam to the hem you prefer — ankle or floor.

Still unsure? Send your height and usual shirt size on WhatsApp — we will confirm before shipping.

Your data

Privacy notice

We collect only what we need to deliver your order, and we never sell it.

What we keep

For an order: your name, phone, wilaya, commune and the items. Optionally your email, for the confirmation. With an account: your email and a protected (hashed) password. We also count page views and bag additions, without cookies and without names.

Who sees it

The store team, and the delivery company you choose (name, phone and address only). Nothing is used for advertising.

On your device

Your bag, language and delivery details are saved in your browser so you don't retype them. A sign-in cookie keeps you logged in. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Under Algerian law 18-07 you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Message us on WhatsApp or through the Contact page.
