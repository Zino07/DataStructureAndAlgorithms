# Brag plan: Kaser Abd Alaziz (https://kaser-abd-alaziz.com/)

**Input:** website. **Tone:** `polished` (calm, elegant, long holds, soft fades). **Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 20 s.

## Rubric
- **What is it?** An Algerian men's thobe house (Noor): thobes, djellabas and shirts, hand-finished, sizes 52–58.
- **Who/what for?** Men shopping for Friday prayer, Eid and the family majlis. The site removes the two worries of buying a thobe online: fit and trust.
- **What sets it apart?** Sizing by height (not chest), cash on delivery in all 58 wilayas, 7-day size exchange.
- **Best claim:** "The thobe, perfected."
- **Visual hook:** the site's own giant Fraunces headline over a hollow outlined "THOBE" watermark on warm cream-and-gold gradients.
- **Real UI shown:** the occasion cards with the real product photos, and the real size table (52–58).
- **Share caption:** "The thobe, perfected. Sizes 52–58, cash on delivery across Algeria."

## Visual identity (from the site CSS)
Cream `#f5f1e8`, ink `#201814`, forest green `#2f4f43`, gold `#8a6d3b`. Fraunces (display, italic accent) + Inter Tight. Outlined watermark words. Fonts and photos are the site's own files, copied to `work/site/`.

## Sound
120 BPM (0.5 s per beat), A minor: Am | F | C | G | Am | F | C | G | C | C. Soft plucks + pad from the first frame, soft kick and hat enter on the first cut, little pluck "ticks" on each element and a quiet filtered whoosh on each scene change. Everything is synthesised on one beat grid counted from frame 0. Every cut and every item reveal lands on a beat.
