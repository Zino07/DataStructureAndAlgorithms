## Look
Landscape clips are cropped to the table and hand (1200×714 → 1080 wide, crisp, feathered edges); portrait clips sit in a phone frame (1.35×). Background is a blurred, beat-pulsing copy of the footage so every ring colour floods the frame. A gold action ticker on the bottom stacks the last three words.

## Sound
144 BPM, A minor: 16th-note pluck arp, pulsing bass, kick every beat, claps on 2 & 4, hats, a one-beat drop before the title, riser into a sub-boom + crash on the title. Every slam word has a thump + tick, every cut a short whoosh. All on the same beat grid from frame 0.
