# Brag plan: personal-finance app (Android), "Aurora: theme as hero"

**Input:** two portrait screen recordings (864×1920, 24 fps). Light Bento theme and dark Aurora Glass theme. Demo data, shown as-is.
**Tone:** polished / premium. **Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 20 s.

## Rubric
- **What is it?** A personal-finance app for Android: Income Radar (balance, last 7 days, left to spend today), Activity, Deep Stats with a Money clock, Accounts, quick New entry.
- **Who/what for?** People who want to see their money at a glance and know what they can still spend today (SAR).
- **What sets it apart?** The look. Aurora Glass is a dark, glowing glass theme where the accent you pick (Lime, Solar, Ember, Lilac) lights the whole app, plus a clean light Bento style. Also the Money clock: earned and spent *per hour*.
- **Best claim:** "Your money, in a new light."
- **Visual hook:** a light screen snaps to dark glass on the first beat, then every beat the accent swaps and its glow floods the whole frame.
- **Real UI shown:** Settings ▸ Look (the real accent taps), Income Radar, Deep Stats ▸ Money clock (+139.98 earned / −65.40 spent per hour), Where it goes / Recent, Your Accounts.
- **Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.
- **App name:** not visible in the footage. The end card leaves room for the name and icon from the APK.

## Visual identity (from the footage)
Accents sampled from the UI: Lime `#d4ff3b`, Solar `#ffe63b`, Ember `#ff7b30`, Lilac `#c5b3ff`. Near-black glass panels on a glowing gradient. Headlines copy the app's own two-weight heading ("Income" light / "Radar" semibold) in Inter Display Light + SemiBold.

## Sound
120 BPM. A beat is 0.5 s, exactly 12 frames at 24 fps. Every cut lands on a beat. Progression per 2-s bar: Gmaj7 | A | F#m7 | Bm7 | Gmaj7 | A | F#m7 | Bm7 | Gmaj7 | D.
- **Bar 0:** a quiet music box.
- **Beat 1:** the "light switch". A click, a sub drop and a shimmer as the screen goes dark, then the full groove: kick, off-beat hats, bass and pad.
- **Hook beats:** a rising chime on each accent swap.
- **Cuts:** a soft whoosh under each cut.
- **Into the outro:** a riser, then an impact and a held chord on the outro downbeat (beat 32).
