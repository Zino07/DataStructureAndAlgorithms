# Brag plan v2: finance app, "Money apps are grey."

**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 20 s, 144 BPM (1 beat = 10 frames, 48 beats). Real UI from the two recordings, demo data as-is. App name not in the footage (APK not received), so the end card carries the tagline only.

## Concept (why v1 was generic)
v1 was a phone in a card under a slogan. v2 is built on one joke and one idea: **grey money apps → this one glows**, and the app's own numbers are the typography.

## Storyboard (beats)
| Beats | Scene | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | Grey | White Bento screen, "Money apps / are grey." Dry ticks. A riser builds. |
| 4–8 | Flip | The real Bento→Aurora toggle fires on the drop. "This one / **glows.**" Lilac → Ember → Solar → Lime, one swap per beat. A ripple blooms from each tapped swatch and the word recolours. |
| 8–16 | Answer | "Can I afford it?" Tilted 3D phone scrolls Income Radar. At beat 11 the phone flies off and **256.52** counts up on a radar sweep. The real "Left to spend" card floats in. "Yes. Go ahead." |
| 16–28 | Money clock | **+139.98** earned and **−65.40** spent per hour count up, over a radar sweep. Then "Your money has a clock." with the real Money clock and Net/Runway cards. "The math is done." |
| 28–40 | Exploded view | "Pick a color. Watch it follow." Phone plus four real UI cards fly out in 3D. Accent cycles Ember → Solar → Lime → Lilac (hue-rotated footage) every 3 beats. |
| 40–48 | Outro | Four-colour aurora. "Money, in color." Three phones (Bento, Lilac, Ember), accent dots tick in, "Aurora Glass · Bento · For Android". |

## Sound
A minor, Am9 | Fmaj7 | Cmaj9 | G6/9. A grey wood-block/muzak bar 0, riser, **drop on beat 4** (sub boom, crash, chimes). Kick-pumped pads, 16th plucks, clap, hats. A break right before the answer. Rising/falling counter ticks (earned up, spent down). Outro crash and held chord. About −14 LUFS.

## Privacy
Status bar and system nav are cropped off. All numbers are demo data.
